# Changelog

## v0.1.0-beta1

- First beta for World of Warcraft: Forever.
- The UI hides like Alt+Z out of combat and comes back in combat, with a living target, or while a window is open. Esc brings it back until something changes.
- The world map, the loot window and bags show on their own out of combat. Chat works with the UI hidden and fades 5 seconds after typing. Tooltips, the breath bar and quest progress text always show.
- Your arrow on the world map is hidden.
- Options panel (`/dui-options`) and commands: `/dui-hide`, `/dui-target`, `/dui-solo`, `/dui-arrow`, `/dui-status`, `/dui-help`.
